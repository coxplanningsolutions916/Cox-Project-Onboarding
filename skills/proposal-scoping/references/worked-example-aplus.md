# Worked Example — A-Plus Screening Tool (productized, subscription)

A productized engagement scoped under Mode 1, spanning DDL Step 1a (the subscription screening tool) and Step 1b (per-property ISRs). Illustrates subscription pricing, the marketing-funnel logic, and a fee backup against the real cost floor. Pricing figures here are recommended starting points pending Chris's confirmation, not committed numbers.

## A — Engagement type

Client: A-Plus Construction (contact Olga Suprun; CEO oversees property sourcing). The screening tool is **Step 1a** (subscription, Cox-owned, mass-market; A-Plus as the anchor client). Per-property ISRs are **Step 1b** (fixed fee). New Task Order under the existing executed agreement (behind TO-1, the T Street ISR, 2026-05-20).

## B — Scope of work

- **1.1 Screening tool — City of Sacramento.** Address/APN lookup returning zoning, General Plan/community/specific-plan designation, overlays, streamlined levers (SB 79 TOD bracket, AB 2097 parking), local ADU caps, vacant-lot identification, and flag logic (e.g., >50-year structure or historic register → cultural screen). Exportable filtered property/mailing list. Built on the ISR analytical framework (the T Street ISR is the template).
- **1.2 Add-on jurisdictions — Sacramento County, Placer/Roseville.** Same logic; local reaction layered on the constant state-law base.
- **1.3 Environmental constraints overlay** — FEMA, NWI, CARI from public data.
- *Drawn down separately (Step 1b):* per-property ISRs at the standard **$2,500**; reserve $1,000 for genuinely small or simple parcels only, batched by neighborhood/zone.

The product ladder maps onto the revised Step 1: instant app lookup (1a) → paid ISR (1b) → Executive Underwrite (Step 2) and permitting. The tool qualifies leads and sells the ISR; the ISR sells the entitlement work.

## C — Budget and fee backup (internal)

Cost floor, City of Sacramento (first build):

| Role | Hours | Cash basis | Cash cost | MSRP value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GIS Analyst/CAD Designer | 10–20 | $40/hr | $400–$800 | $1,500–$3,000 |
| Principal (logic mapping + QA; largely pre-built) | 3–6 | owner time | nominal | $750–$1,500 |

Each add-on jurisdiction: 5–10 GIS hours → $200–$400 cash. Build cost is near-zero per additional client once a jurisdiction exists — the resale margin is the point.

## C/E — Subscription pricing (recommended; Chris to confirm)

Priced as a funnel: near cost-plus-modest-margin to attract and qualify, with margin downstream in ISRs and entitlement work.

| Tier | Monthly | Annual (≈2 months free) |
|---|---|---|
| Free trial | 14 days or ~5 lookups, single jurisdiction, no export | — |
| Standard (single jurisdiction) | ~$150 | ~$1,500 |
| Regional (all jurisdictions) | ~$300 | ~$3,000 |

A-Plus founding/anchor rate: regional locked at ~$2,000/year for year one in exchange for reference/case-study rights. Per-property ISRs (Step 1b) billed separately at the standard $2,500; the T Street ISR's $1,000 was an intro rate below cost (the report was substantial). Reserve $1,000 for genuinely small parcels only.

Gating rationale: $150/month filters non-serious users and is trivial against a development deal, while signaling real value. Free trial drives top-of-funnel once the tool is live.

## D — Timeline

Demo within ~3–5 days of signature (so A-Plus can show their CEO); City of Sacramento live in 1–2 weeks; all three jurisdictions within ~3–4 weeks. Nothing material outside Cox's control — built from public and state data.

## E — Payment / billing

Annual subscription invoiced once via Harvest for the anchor client until recurring-billing infrastructure (Productive or equivalent) is built for the mass-market rollout. Per-property ISRs invoiced per order.
