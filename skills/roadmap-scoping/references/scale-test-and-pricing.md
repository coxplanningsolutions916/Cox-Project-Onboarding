# Scale test and pricing (decided Sept 30, 2026)

## The three rungs

| Rung | Price | Includes | Session | Credit |
|------|-------|----------|---------|--------|
| Snapshot | $500 | Zoning and General Plan read; constraint screen (wetlands, flood, species, cultural flags); one-page go or no-go; the three questions to answer next | none | 100% credited to a Roadmap ordered within 60 days |
| Roadmap | $2,500 | Snapshot content plus: what to build, how it gets approved (the approval set and bodies), permit stack, schedule, order-of-magnitude cost ranges with what firms each up, historic-aerial exhibit, graphic schedule | one 2-hour senior planner review and working session | none; closes with a priced Step 1 or 2 proposal |
| Roadmap Plus | $5,000 | Roadmap content plus a board-ready one-page summary and a sub-consultant read where the scale test triggered it (biology, engineering, cultural) | two sessions | none |

The Roadmap price is fixed. Promotions (Q1 2027: free Snapshot, capped at 20) give away the Snapshot, never the Roadmap. Nothing is sold below $500; the free intake screen remains the lead magnet.

## The scale test (three questions on the intake form; the model answers what it can)

1. **Parcels and jurisdictions.** More than one parcel, or more than one jurisdiction (city plus county, or any special district with its own approval), is a Plus.
2. **Resources.** Any mapped waters, wetlands, 100-year flood zone, or listed-species habitat on or adjacent to the site is a Plus, because the memo then has to carry a federal or state permit pathway and a mitigation discussion.
3. **Entitlement.** A use allowed by right with only ministerial approvals is a Roadmap. A discretionary entitlement (rezone, General Plan amendment, use permit with CEQA exposure, subdivision map) is a Plus when combined with either of the first two; alone it stays a Roadmap.

Any yes on question 2, or more than one parcel or jurisdiction, is a Plus. Everything else is a Roadmap. A Snapshot is sold when the client wants only the go or no-go, or as the promotion; it can always be upgraded.

Edge cases go to Chris. Do not downgrade a Plus to win the sale; the extra session and the sub-consultant read are what keep the memo honest on a complex site.
