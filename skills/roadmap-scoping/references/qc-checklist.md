# Roadmap QC checklist — the senior planner's gate (2 hours, included in the fee)

Work top to bottom with the memo and the screen side by side. Any unchecked item blocks the send.

## Facts and sources
- [ ] Every row of the property table has a source in the screen (parcel viewer, zoning map, General Plan, Acres report, NWI/CARI query with URLs). Rows without one read `[FACT NEEDED]` and are listed in section 8.
- [ ] No plan or program membership (specific plan, HCP/NCCP, overlay) is asserted without a map or GIS citation with a date. (Riego R1: two such claims in a delivered report were wrong and cost a framework each.)
- [ ] The General Plan relied on is adopted and in effect; an unadopted plan is not a basis for tiering or precedent.
- [ ] The parking rule, density bonus tiers, and fee schedules cited are the current ones, with effective dates.
- [ ] Mapped versus field-confirmed is stated for every constraint. Nothing client-reported is presented as Cox's finding.

## Structure and numbers
- [ ] The bottom line is three lines a client can repeat, and the body supports each one.
- [ ] Every cost that rests on an unresolved input is a range with the resolver named under it. Nothing the project cannot yet know carries a bracket; it carries drivers and reference unit costs instead (R5).
- [ ] The approval set names the approval body for each action and whether a federal or state permit enters the picture; if one does, the structural consequence (ESA consultation, 401 certification, CEQA pathway shift) is stated (R2).
- [ ] Field tasks are placed inside their season windows and the schedule says which window governs (R3).
- [ ] The total to entitlement names its carrying assumption in the same sentence.
- [ ] The scale test result matches the rung sold; if the screen moved it to Plus, Chris has approved the change before the memo goes out.

## Client-facing hygiene
- [ ] No hours, rates, roles, margin, vendor names, or internal reasoning anywhere in the memo.
- [ ] The planning-purposes header and the Terms non-guarantee are verbatim.
- [ ] Chris's voice per `cox-document-formatting`: plain words, no dashes as asides, no exclamation marks, no buzzwords; first-person plural.
- [ ] Owner names, APNs, and site details appear only in the client copy; any marketing reuse is anonymized and consented.
- [ ] Figures carry their attribution (Google Earth imagery line) and the "desktop review; field screening confirms" caption.
- [ ] The next step names one proposal and what it resolves from section 8.

## Stop and escalate
- [ ] Anything touching the Clean Water Act 404(f) agricultural exemption, or any wording that could read as concealing a resource from an agency: stop; Chris decides.
- [ ] A recommendation that depends on a Williamson Act contract, a LAFCo action, a wastewater path, or a federal nexus the screen could not confirm: flag in section 8 and in the cover email.
